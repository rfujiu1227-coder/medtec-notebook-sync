MEDTEC Notebook Sync

MEDTEC Notebook Sync is a small personal tool used by a single student to organise his own lecture materials.

It runs on the owner's own computer. When a new lecture transcript appears in the owner's Google Drive, the tool adds it as a source to the matching notebook in the owner's Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM) account, using Google's official Gemini Notebook API.

The tool is not offered to the public and has no other users.
